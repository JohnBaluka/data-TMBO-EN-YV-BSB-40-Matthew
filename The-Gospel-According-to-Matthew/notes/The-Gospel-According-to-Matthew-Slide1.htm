The book itself does not say who wrote it.

From very early on, the church attributed it to Matthew, the tax collector whom Jesus called away from his customs table.

Whoever held the pen wrote for readers who knew the Hebrew Scriptures. Again and again the story pauses to say that something happened to fulfill what the prophets had said.

And the aim is clear from the first line to the last: Jesus is the promised King, the Son of David, sent now to all nations.
