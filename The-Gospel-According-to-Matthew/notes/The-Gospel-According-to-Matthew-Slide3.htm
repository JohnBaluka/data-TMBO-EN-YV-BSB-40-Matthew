Matthew gathers the teaching of Jesus into five great blocks, and closes each one with the same kind of line: when Jesus had finished saying these things.

First, the Sermon on the Mount, chapters five to seven.

Second, the sending out of the Twelve, chapter ten.

Third, the parables of the kingdom, chapter thirteen.

Fourth, life together among His followers, chapter eighteen.

And fifth, the end of the age, chapters twenty-four and twenty-five.
