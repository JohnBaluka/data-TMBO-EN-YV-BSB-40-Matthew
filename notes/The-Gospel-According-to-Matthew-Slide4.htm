The first thing we hear Jesus preach in Matthew is a blessing:

"Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven"

And that phrase – the kingdom of heaven – will become one of the defining themes of Matthew’s Gospel. In fact, it appears

Thirty-two times throughout the book.

When Jesus begins his public ministry, his message is clear:

"Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near."

The kingdom of heaven is Matthew’s way of describing God’s reign, and in this book that reign arrives in person.
