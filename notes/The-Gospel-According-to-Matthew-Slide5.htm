Matthews story begins and ends with a promise. Near the beginning, in Matthew chapter one, we are told:

“And they will call Him Immanual” – which means, “god with us.”

Then, at the very end of the book, the risen Jesus leaves his disciples with these words:

“And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age”.

From the beginning of Matthew to the very last words of Jesus, the promise remains the same:

God with us.

Matthew begins with Immanuel - and ends with “I am with you always.”

And everything in between shows us what it means for God to be with us.

From His coming to His mission, Jesus is with His people.
