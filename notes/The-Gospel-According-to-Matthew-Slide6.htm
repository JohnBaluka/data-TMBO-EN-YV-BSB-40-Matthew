Here is how the rest of this series works.

There is one deck for each of Matthew’s twenty-eight chapters, and this introduction.

Every verse is there, in the Berean Standard Bible, which is public domain.

Each verse appears on its own click, and only the verses are read aloud. Verse numbers, headings and footnotes stay in the notes.

And the slide titles are the translators’ own section headings, not ours.
